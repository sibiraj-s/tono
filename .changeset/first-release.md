First release of Tono.

- Search YouTube and YouTube Music, paste a link, or pick an audio file from your phone
- Trim on a waveform with fade in/out and volume leveling, and loop the clip while you trim
- Set as ringtone, notification, alarm, or for one or more contacts
- Hear a tone as a pretend incoming call before you set it
- My tones: rename, delete and change where each tone is used
- Ringtone rotation: switch between your tones daily or weekly
- Check for updates and install them from inside the app
